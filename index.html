Why TIMBR Field

Purpose-built beats general-purpose.

Most consultancies run their field work on paper forms, spreadsheets, or generic data-capture apps that were never designed for arboriculture. That gap costs hours in re-typing — and it shows in the data.

Paper forms & generic tools

  • Field notes re-typed back at the office — hours lost, errors introduced
  • Form builders that don't know what a TPZ, DSH or SULE is
  • Photos stranded in camera rolls, disconnected from the tree record
  • Your project data locked in a vendor's own cloud database
  • One bar of signal and the whole capture workflow stalls

With TIMBR Field

  • Structured workflows that mirror how consulting arborists actually assess
  • Every field captured once, correctly — species, dimensions, health, structure, retention
  • Photos taken in-app, GPS-tagged and attached to the right tree
  • Assessments sync into your SharePoint site, ready for reporting
  • Offline-first: capture never waits for signal

Built for the field

Everything a site inspection needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Guided, step-by-step workflows take each tree from arrival to a complete, validated record — even ten metres past the last bar of reception.

Structured assessment workflows

Species, dimensions, age class, health, structure, SULE, retention value and recommendations — captured in a guided sequence with sensible pick-lists, so records come back complete and consistent across every consultant on your team.

  • Step-by-step project flow, from site context to per-tree assessment
  • Recommended works recorded against each tree, ready for the report
  • Searchable species library, botanical and common names

Standards-aligned, by design

TIMBR Field speaks the language of AS 4970. Reference TPZ and SRZ calculations are computed from your DSH and DARB measurements as you type — clearly labelled as reference figures, with the clause they come from.

  • Notional root zone and structural root zone reference calcs in-app
  • Terminology and fields that map onto AS 4970 reporting
  • Council audit workflows for scheduled works verification

Offline-first. Sync when ready.

Every entry is written to a local queue on the device the moment you save it. No signal on site? Keep working — the queue holds everything and syncs it into your SharePoint tenant the moment you're back in range. Field crews are never blocked by coverage.

  • Full capture capability with zero connectivity
  • Clear per-project status — see what's queued and what's synced
  • Photos and records travel together, nothing left behind

Photo + GPS capture

Site and tree photos taken in-app are GPS-tagged and attached to the record they belong to — evidence that holds up when the report is questioned.

Project & client context

Client, address, council, report type and inspection date travel with every assessment, so office-side reporting starts with everything in place.

Microsoft 365-native

No new platform for IT to assess. TIMBR Field authenticates with your Microsoft 365 accounts and writes to SharePoint lists and libraries you already govern.

Data ownership

Your data. Your tenant. Full stop.

Most field apps sync your project data into their own cloud database — and you rent access to it. TIMBR Field doesn't. Every assessment writes directly into your organisation's Microsoft 365 / SharePoint tenant, under infrastructure you already control, govern and trust.

Assessments flow from the device straight into SharePoint. No middle database, no vendor silo.

Under governance you already run

Permissions, retention, auditing, backup — every control your organisation applies to SharePoint applies to your tree data automatically. Nothing new to certify.

Trusted by your IT team on day one

Sign-in is your existing Microsoft 365 identity. For councils and enterprise clients, "where does the data live?" has a one-word answer: yours.

No lock-in, ever

Your records are SharePoint lists and files in standard formats. Stop using TIMBR tomorrow and you keep everything, exactly where it already is.

One source of truth

Field capture, office reporting and spatial work all read and write the same records in the same tenant — no exports, no version drift.
