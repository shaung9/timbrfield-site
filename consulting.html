Licence · ConsultingIn beta

From the tree to the impact assessment, typed once

The Consulting licence is where TIMBR started: an arborist's own tools for site assessments and the reports that follow them. Trees are captured on the phone, managed in the Hub and turned into the impact assessment from the same records, without retyping anything in between.

On site

TIMBR Field walks each tree from arrival to a finished record, in the order you already work.

  • Species, dimensions, health, structure, SULE and retention in a guided form
  • DSH and multi-stemmed trees combined the way AS 4970:2025 does it, with NRZ and SRZ shown as you type
  • Photos taken in the app, tagged with GPS and attached to the right tree
  • A whole site assessed with no reception; it syncs when you are back in range

Everything Field does on site

In the office

Projects and clients

Each job as a project with its client, site, trees and status, so you can see which reports are ready to go out.

Trees on a map

Every captured tree plotted where it stands, with its assessment and reference figures one click away.

Impact assessment reports

The QGIS plugin brings in the development plans, sets your trees against the proposal and produces the Arboricultural Impact Assessment from the data collected on site.

Who it suits

Consulting arborists and consultancies writing tree reports, impact assessments, protection plans and risk assessments for councils, builders and homeowners. One person or a team; every consultant records a tree the same way.

One Hub, whichever licences you hold

Everyone installs the same TIMBR Hub. You will see every module from the first day; the ones you have not licensed sit there slightly greyed out, to look at but not use. Add a licence later and that module switches on over the same records, with nothing to move or migrate.

  • One download for Mac and Windows, one phone app
  • Licences for Consulting, Contract Operations and Domestic Quoting, on their own or together
  • All of it stored in your own Microsoft 365 and SharePoint, never in a database of ours
