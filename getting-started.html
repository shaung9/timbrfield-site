TIMBR Field › Getting started

For whoever looks after your Microsoft 365

Set TIMBR up for your company

One-off, about thirty minutes, done once for the organisation. After it, each person installs the app and signs in with their own Microsoft 365 account; an administrator is only needed again to give a new person access to the site.

In one line

Delegated permissions only (User.Read, Sites.Selected), no secrets or certificates, and access to one SharePoint site that you create and grant. Nothing else in the tenant. TIMBR cannot do anything when nobody is signed in, and every action appears in your sign-in logs as a named person.

App registration: 63686792-1aa5-4dc4-9d27-2b2a215a7376, published by SG Arboricultural Consulting.

Step 1. Create a dedicated SharePoint site

A new, empty Team site, set to Private, for example https://yourtenant.sharepoint.com/sites/TIMBR (SharePoint admin centre › Sites › Active sites › Create › Team site).

Not the tenant's main (root) site, and not an existing team site. A dedicated site is what lets TIMBR be confined to it in step 4, and keeps its lists and photos apart from everything else.

Step 2. Give your people access to that site

Add everyone who will use TIMBR as members of the site. Make the person who will connect first an owner: creating TIMBR's lists on the site (step 6) needs owner rights.

TIMBR can only reach what a person can reach, so site membership is how you decide who can use it. A new starter later is just a new member.

Step 3. Consent to the app

A Global Administrator consents for the organisation, either by signing in to TIMBR once and ticking Consent on behalf of your organisation, or in Entra › Enterprise applications › TIMBR › Permissions › Grant admin consent.

The consent screen should list exactly User.Read and Sites.Selected. If it lists anything else, stop and tell us.

Step 4. Grant the app that one site

With PnP PowerShell, signed in as a SharePoint administrator:

Connect-PnPOnline -Url "https://yourtenant.sharepoint.com/sites/TIMBR" -Interactive
Grant-PnPAzureADAppSitePermission -AppId "63686792-1aa5-4dc4-9d27-2b2a215a7376" -DisplayName "TIMBR" -Site "https://yourtenant.sharepoint.com/sites/TIMBR" -Permissions Manage

Manage rather than Write because TIMBR creates its own lists on first run, which Write does not allow. It applies to this one site only. If PnP PowerShell is not yet set up in the tenant, it needs registering once: Register-PnPEntraIDApp -ApplicationName "PnP Management Shell" -Tenant yourtenant.onmicrosoft.com -Interactive.

Step 5. Start the free trial

Open TIMBR Hub and sign in with your Microsoft 365 account. The Hub says the organisation has no licence yet and points you to its Licence page: type your company's name and press Start a free trial. The trial begins at once, covers everyone in the organisation, and the Licence page shows the day it ends. The only things sent are your organisation's tenant ID and the company name you typed. One trial per organisation; when it ends, or to go straight to a subscription, email hello@timbrfield.com.au with the tenant ID the Licence page shows.

Step 6. Connect and create the lists

In TIMBR Hub, as the site owner from step 2: Deployment › paste the full site address › Connect › sign in › Provision SharePoint Lists. This creates TIMBR's lists and libraries on the site. It is safe to run again later; it only ever adds what is missing.

Then, per person

Hub (Mac or Windows): install, open Deployment, paste the full site address, press Connect, sign in with their own account.

Field (iPhone, iPad, Android): install from the beta invitation; on the sign-in screen enter the same site address under Your TIMBR site, then sign in.

Checking it afterwards

Run Get-PnPAzureADAppSitePermission against any other site: TIMBR should not appear.

Questions IT usually asks

Can it read our other SharePoint sites, OneDrive, mail or directory?

No. It asks for Sites.Selected and User.Read only, and Sites.Selected reaches only sites explicitly granted. TIMBR connects only to the site address it is given; it never searches for sites or falls back to any other site.

Can it do anything when nobody is signed in?

No. No application permissions, no credentials. Every action appears in your sign-in logs as a named person.

How do we revoke it?

Entra › Enterprise applications › TIMBR › Delete, or Revoke-PnPAzureADAppSitePermission. Access stops at once; the data stays in your SharePoint.

Conditional Access and MFA?

Supported and recommended. TIMBR signs in through Microsoft's own page and honours your policies.

Does it need a service account?

No.

What leaves our tenant?

The tenant ID, to check the licence, and the company name you type when you start the trial. Optional features you switch on yourself may send more: online quote acceptance sends a quote's number, total and line descriptions to a page the client opens; drive-time routing sends map coordinates. Each is off until you turn it on, and Your data sets out all of it.
