Licence · Domestic QuotingComing soon

Quote residential tree work on site, priced from your own rates

Domestic Quoting is for tree businesses working for homeowners. Enter how long the job will take on site and TIMBR prices it from your rates. The quote goes out from your own mailbox, the client accepts online, and the job carries on through scheduling, invoicing and payment in the same Hub.

Coming soon. Domestic Quoting is still being tested end to end before anyone else uses it on a real job. Join the waitlist and you will hear when it opens.

How a job will run

  1. EnquiryThe job is logged with the client's details and the address.
  2. Quote on siteEnter time on site and any travel or gear; the price comes from your own rates.
  3. SendA branded PDF quote goes from your own mailbox, with a link the client can open on their phone.
  4. Accept onlineThe client sees the work and the total and accepts or declines, and the answer comes back to the Hub.
  5. ScheduleAccepted jobs go onto the crew board for the day and truck you choose.
  6. Invoice and get paidMark it done, send the invoice and record the payment.

Built for the way quotes actually change

  • A sent quote is fixed; any change becomes a new version that has to be sent again
  • The client's link always shows exactly what they were sent
  • Your own rates, travel and equipment, set once in settings

One Hub, whichever licences you hold

Everyone installs the same TIMBR Hub. You will see every module from the first day; the ones you have not licensed sit there slightly greyed out, to look at but not use. Add a licence later and that module switches on over the same records, with nothing to move or migrate.

  • One download for Mac and Windows, one phone app
  • Licences for Consulting, Contract Operations and Domestic Quoting, on their own or together
  • All of it stored in your own Microsoft 365 and SharePoint, never in a database of ours
