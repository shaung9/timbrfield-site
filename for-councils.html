The questions your IT and records people will ask

If you work in local government, the software is the easy part. Getting it past procurement, records management and information security is the real job. Here is the short version, so you can forward this page instead of writing an email.

Where does the data actually sit?

In your tenant, in your SharePoint site, in the region your Microsoft 365 tenant is already provisioned in. We do not copy it anywhere else and we do not keep a copy. If your tenant is in Australia, your tree data is in Australia.

How does this fit our records obligations?

Assessments are SharePoint list items and library files, so whatever retention labels, disposal schedules and classification your records team already applies can be applied to them. Nothing sits outside your recordkeeping system waiting to be discovered later.

What if TIMBR goes away?

A fair question to ask a small vendor, and we would rather answer it than dodge it. If we disappeared tomorrow, every assessment, photo and report stays in your tenant in readable formats and your team keeps working. That is not a promise, it is how the architecture works.

Who are we buying from?

SG Arboricultural Consulting, an Australian arboricultural consultancy based in Melbourne. You can call the number below and speak to the person who builds it and uses it on his own jobs.

Built for the way council work actually runs

  • Audit workflows for checking scheduled works, so what was recommended and what was done stay connected.
  • AS 4970:2025 terminology throughout, including the NRZ and TPZ split, so records match the standard your planners are assessing against. What changed in 2025.
  • Consistent records across contractors. Every consultant records a tree the same way, which matters when several are working across one municipality.
  • Photographic evidence tied to trees and locations, which is what you need when a decision is questioned later.
  • Works offline, because reserves and rural roads do not have reception.

For procurement

Need something more formal for a procurement pack? Ask and we will put it in writing, including data handling, hosting arrangements and business details. We would rather answer the hard questions early than have them surface halfway through an assessment.
